# Supplier research and data provenance — 5 November 2026

These are six real-company research examples, not onboarded partners. There is no API integration, live inventory synchronization or order forwarding. The selection is a representative catalogue sample, not an exhaustive copy of every product they sell. Three are presented as established names and three as Zambia-focused suppliers; this is not a verified company-size ranking or a claim that the latter are small businesses.

Regular opening schedules exclude special holiday changes and can become stale. The interface distinguishes official published schedules from directory snapshots, and links each hours source. Storefront area pins were chosen for the demonstration from the stated area; they are approximate and not geocoded/verified entrances.

Published Micmar snapshot prices included: FG81050 wheelbarrow K2,970; FG00415 shovel K445; TG1302306 grinder K2,620; THTRS3101 straps K450; THT261625 tool bag K185; RG7900K generator K18,840; RG-2700 generator K10,250; PBH2000RE rotary hammer K1,700. Retailer confirmation is required before real spending. All other application prices, stock, weights, transport, labour, platform fees and rental rates are simulation assumptions.

No supplier product descriptions, photos or logos were copied. Product/category facts are paraphrased and source links retained. Product artwork uses original illustrations and the company-name badges are plain typography, not reproductions of their logos.
