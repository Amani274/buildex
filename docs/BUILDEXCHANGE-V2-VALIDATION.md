# Current validation — operational prototype update, 5 November 2026

## Automated verification

**36 Django integration/workflow tests passed** on Windows, Python 3.13.15, Django 5.2.19, DRF 3.18.3 and the isolated SQLite test database. They cover the original stock/quotation/order/delivery/site-receipt/rental/private-evidence flows plus:

- Server-derived fees and destination-aware checkout replay; client totals cannot replace the amount charged.
- Terms acceptance, bank-session ownership, insufficient fictitious funds, expiration, declined retry and approval replay without a second debit.
- Payment-before-preparation enforcement; rental charge plus deposit; full uncollected cancellation refund and deposit-only inspected-return refund.
- Support-only permissions, unrelated-order and banking-data exclusion, response handling and removal of support superuser/staff privileges.
- Active-assignment location checks, explicit consent, coordinate validation, source=demo and authorized recipient tracking.
- Additive six-store/37-product source seeding, preserving edited stock on rerun; removal of raw audit internals from customer payloads.

System checks, migration-drift checks and JavaScript syntax checks were run for the changed application. No PostgreSQL runtime or parallel PostgreSQL concurrency result is claimed.

## Executed browser demonstration

A fictional order from the Chilanga sample storefront was placed through the redesigned buyer basket. Clicking the Lusaka map changed the stored site pin and recalculated the fee estimate. Its final amount was **K443.35**, consisting of K165 material, K230.10 estimated delivery, K40 handling labour and K8.25 platform fee. These are demo rates, not a real retailer quotation.

The local bank simulator approved the transaction and showed a balance change from **K100,000.00 to K99,556.65**, with a recorded receipt. Supplier preparation and driver job acceptance were exercised through their role screens. The driver recorded pickup and transit, consented to the rehearsal and sent a moving series of source=demo positions. The final saved position matched the chosen site pin. Arrival was recorded separately; the supervisor then inspected the delivery and confirmed a good receipt. An order-specific support case was submitted and resolved through the restricted customer-support desk. No receipt was automatically generated by tracking.

The initial standard OSM raster layer displayed blocked tiles. It was replaced with OpenFreeMap vector maps, whose streets and attribution were verified visually. The map renderer was checked against the public advisory database; an initially selected MapLibre release had an advisory and was replaced with **6.4.1**. The final MapLibre/Leaflet package-version check returned no advisories. This is a package-level, point-in-time result, not a proof of overall application security or a full bundled-transitive-dependency audit. Original library licence notices are retained.

The previous procurement-to-discrepancy browser workflow and private-photo controls are preserved in the integration tests. Physical phone GPS, actual bank/provider integration, live traffic/road routing, production hosting and supplier partnerships were not tested or claimed. Photos/product branding from supplier sites were not copied.

## Limits that matter in a presentation

- The full application is local. No new push, PR, Render deployment, payment-provider onboarding or merchant communication occurred.
- Source catalogues and hours are dated research; half of the schedules are directory snapshots and explicitly marked to confirm. Only eight named Micmar product prices are sourced snapshots. Other prices/specifications/stock/weights are clearly demo data.
- Map pins are approximate, movement rehearsal is simulated, and ETA starts after dispatch without live traffic. GPS requires consent, secure browser context and a reachable service on the phone. Real GPS cannot be claimed from a desktop rehearsal.
- Demo bank balances and refunds are local ledger records. Nothing is sent to a bank and no real account credentials are collected.
- Support sees submitted cases and their relevant order details only. Offline operator administration and commercial onboarding/verification remain future work.
- Published-store closing times do not prevent a classroom supplier account from accepting a simulated job. Do not interpret that as evidence of real dispatch outside opening hours.
