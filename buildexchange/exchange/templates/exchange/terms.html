with clear expectations.

These rules describe this student demonstration. They are not a commercial supplier agreement or bank service.

1. Demonstration only

Orders, stock, transport jobs, rental bookings and bank balances in this application are simulated. Do not use it to arrange a real purchase, send real funds or make a real equipment booking. Approving a demo bank payment only changes the local demonstration records.

2. Supplier and catalogue information

Six company names and selected product/category information come from linked public sources. These businesses have not been onboarded as partners. Published prices are dated research snapshots; other prices, stock counts, rental offers and product variants are labelled as demo data. The selection is a sample, not a complete or live supplier catalogue. Confirm real prices, opening hours and stock directly with the business. Holiday hours may differ.

3. Delivery, labour and platform fees

The prototype shows an item subtotal, estimated delivery charge, loading/unloading labour and a 5% demo platform fee before checkout. These are BuildExchange demonstration rules, not a real supplier, labour or transporter tariff. Distances use an approximation from the supplier’s area pin to the site; travel and preparation estimates do not include live traffic. Store area pins are approximate, so the pickup gate must be confirmed.

4. Location and tracking

Maps are displayed through OpenFreeMap using OpenStreetMap data and require internet access. Loading the map sends tile requests, including the viewed map area and your connection IP, to that provider. Click the map to choose a demo site. Device location sharing starts only when the assigned transporter explicitly enables it and grants browser permission. Shared location is visible to authorized order participants and support only when a support case exists. Stop sharing at any time, and it also stops when you leave the tracking screen. GPS requires a secure browser context; ordinary phone access over an insecure local-network URL will not work. A moving vehicle labelled “Demo movement” uses simulated coordinates and is not live GPS.

5. Site inspection and rental returns

Transporter arrival does not confirm goods received. The assigned site supervisor records actual quantities, condition, notes and optional photos. A shortage or damage creates a discrepancy record for follow-up. This is an account-authenticated inspection record, not a certified legal signature or automatic liability decision. Rental deposits and refunds are fictitious; collection, return condition and damage are separate records.

6. Data and support

Use fictional addresses, names, messages and nonsensitive sample photos. Never enter real banking credentials, private identity documents or confidential site details. The local operator controls the demonstration database and backups. Photo metadata is removed on upload; images remain private to authorized users. Customer support can review submitted cases and reply, but cannot buy goods, dispatch jobs, change prices, move funds or create privileged accounts. Commercial retention/deletion and identity policies must be completed before a real pilot.

7. Research and asset credits

Supplier source links are available in each storefront. The BuildExchange logo and product illustrations are original project artwork. The hero illustration was generated for this prototype. MapLibre, OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap attribution is retained. No company logo or product photo has been copied into the demo.
